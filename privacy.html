🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 3, 2025

🎯 Our Commitment: Dots and Boxes Classroom Edition is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal data and prioritize the safety of our users, especially students.

📊 Information We Collect

When You Sign In with Google:

  • Display Name: To show your name to other players
  • Email Address: For account identification (not displayed publicly)
  • Profile Picture: To display your avatar in games (optional)

Game Data:

  • Game Sessions: Moves, scores, and chat messages during gameplay
  • Room Codes: Temporary identifiers for multiplayer games
  • Connection Status: To manage multiplayer sessions

🛡️ How We Protect Your Information

  • Secure Firebase: All data is stored on Google's secure Firebase platform
  • Temporary Storage: Game sessions are automatically deleted after completion
  • No Permanent Records: We don't keep long-term records of your gameplay
  • COPPA Compliant: Safe for educational use with students under 13

🤝 How We Use Your Information

  • Enable multiplayer gaming experiences
  • Display your name and avatar to other players
  • Facilitate in-game chat during sessions
  • Improve game performance and fix bugs

📤 Information Sharing

We do NOT:

  • Sell your personal information to third parties
  • Share your data with advertisers
  • Store unnecessary personal information
  • Track your activity outside the game

🗑️ Data Retention

  • Game Sessions: Deleted within 24 hours of completion
  • Chat Messages: Removed when game sessions end
  • Account Info: Only while you're signed in to Google
  • Room Data: Automatically cleaned up after games

🎓 Educational Use

This game is designed for classroom environments. Teachers and educators should:

  • Review this privacy policy before classroom use
  • Ensure students understand the multiplayer features
  • Monitor chat during gameplay if needed
  • Contact us with any concerns about educational use

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Parental Rights

Parents have the right to:

  • Review what information their child provides
  • Request deletion of their child's information
  • Refuse further collection of their child's information

📱 Your Rights

You can:

  • Sign out at any time to stop data collection
  • Play locally without providing any personal information
  • Contact us to request data deletion
  • Ask questions about our privacy practices

📧 Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or our data practices:

  • Email: privacy@dotsandboxes.education
  • Subject: "Privacy Policy Question"

🔄 Policy Updates

We may update this policy occasionally. When we do:

  • We'll update the "Effective Date" above
  • Significant changes will be highlighted in the game
  • Continued use means acceptance of updates

🌟 Remember: Your privacy matters to us! We've designed this game to be safe, fun, and educational while respecting your personal information.
